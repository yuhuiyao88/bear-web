How the sources work together

Grants.gov supplies opportunity records. NIH priorities and notices provide context. Official faculty biographies establish a starting profile. Publication metadata is a discovery lead and does not establish a specific faculty contribution.

Members can share Google Scholar profile links and CSV exports through the Knowledge & needs board. The prototype does not scrape Scholar. New source access failures remain visible, and an unsuccessful refresh preserves the previous published snapshot.

Next application deadlines, full eligibility, faculty availability, and access to a resource are never assumed from a search match.
